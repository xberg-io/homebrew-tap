## Area Map

Homebrew tap for the xberg-io CLIs.

- `Formula/*.rb` — one formula per product (`formula-maintenance` skill)
- `scripts/build-bottles.sh`, `scripts/verify-bottle-urls.sh` — bottle build and URL checks
- `BOTTLES.md` — bottle hosting notes
- `.github/workflows/` — formula and bottle validation
